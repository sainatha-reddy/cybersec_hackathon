# Evaluation

Generated by `python benchmark/run.py --cases 300 --seed 94000` (300 randomised captures, 356 s).

Each case is a fresh 120-second synthetic capture: office background traffic at a random rate, 0–2 problems with randomised parameters (loss rate, scan speed and size, poisoning interval, outage length, number of forged answers, flood size, ...) placed in separate time slots, and 0–3 *hard negatives*: benign situations that resemble problems. A problem counts as **correct** when it is matched one-to-one to planted packet evidence and has the correct independent verdict. Linked findings receive no automatic credit. Unmatched root and linked findings are counted separately.

## What the benchmark caught during development

The first 60-case trial scored 94% correct verdicts and exposed three weaknesses, which were then fixed:

* **Slow port scans** (about 12 probes/s) landed just below the attack threshold and were marked UNCERTAIN. Fix: the 'ports tried' clue now scales with the number of ports, and a new clue counts probes that hit closed ports or missing hosts.
* **Short DNS outages** were diluted by healthy traffic in the rest of the capture (failure ratio 23% overall). Fix: the failure ratio is measured over the worst 30-second window.
* **Busy clients** (many servers, all carrying data) produced a needless 'many connection attempts' incident. Fix: such sources are no longer reported unless they use stealth probe types.

## Honest caveats

* The captures are synthetic and written with the same packet primitives as the bundled samples, so this measures robustness to parameter changes, timing, background noise and combinations, not every quirk of real networks.
* Four named public Wireshark captures are independently reproducible using `python benchmark/real_captures.py --fetch`. Source URLs and SHA-256 hashes are pinned in `benchmark/public-captures.json`; observed results are in `docs/public-capture-results.json`. The capture files are not redistributed. This small check does not establish production-network accuracy.
